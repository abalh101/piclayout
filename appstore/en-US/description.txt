Make polished photo collages with PicLayout. Arrange up to 12 photos in Grid or Freestyle, adjust the composition, and export a high-resolution PNG or JPEG.

Start with a built-in design or let Smart Auto Layout recommend a grid based on the shapes of your photos and the format you choose. Adjust dividers in supported grids, build a custom layout, or move and rotate photos freely. Choose a social media format or set your own aspect ratio.

Fine-tune color, light, crop, and filters. Add text, stickers, and shapes, manage layers, and explore backgrounds, patterns, and blur in Style Studio.

Save and reopen editable projects on your device. Export a .piclayout archive to move a project, save a collage to Photos, or use the iOS share sheet. The editing tools work offline, and the example projects let you explore without selecting personal photos.
