# Age rating and compliance declarations

## Source-backed questionnaire guidance

- PicLayout is an offline photo-editing utility with bundled original abstract sample images, ordinary sticker labels and decorative shapes.
- The app has no in-app web browser, feeds, chat, account profiles, public uploads, ads, purchases, gambling, loot boxes, graphic violence, sexual content, drugs, or horror themes. The only web link opens the privacy policy in the system browser.
- Users can edit private photos and captions. The app has no hosted user-content feature. The user may hand a file to an external destination through the system share sheet. Confirm Apple’s current questionnaire definition of user-generated content for this workflow as part of completing the form.
- The source is consistent with a low rating, potentially 4+. PicLayout has no shared-location or dating and relationships features. Apple computes the final value and region-specific ratings. Complete the live questionnaire using the signed build and your distribution choices. Use Photo & Video as the primary category. [Age rating questions and values](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/age-ratings-values-and-definitions/), [new content descriptors](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=yxkh08us).

## Publisher declarations

- **Content rights.** Demo art and logo are generated by the repository’s original generators. Verify you own or have rights to distribute the branding, source assets, dependencies, and any new material used in screenshots. Confirm the content-rights answer as publisher. The owner must supply the correct copyright holder.
- **Encryption.** PicLayout does not provide encrypted projects, protected transport to a PicLayout service, or a password workflow. It writes unencrypted ZIP archives and rejects encrypted entries. The linked Dart `archive` package includes optional AES/ZipCrypto code and platform components can use OS cryptography. Review the final binary and distribution territories before answering Apple’s export compliance questions. The project omits `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption` so App Store Connect can ask for your determination. Complete Manage Compliance for TestFlight and provide documentation if Apple requests it. [Apple export compliance](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/overview-of-export-compliance).
- **EU trader status.** Decide your status based on the purpose and circumstances under which you distribute PicLayout. For EU availability, enter your status in App Store Connect and validate the address, phone, and email required for a trader when applicable. A free app alone does not determine classification. [Apple EU DSA instructions](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-compliance-information/manage-european-union-digital-services-act-trader-requirements/).
- **App privacy and website.** Confirm the recommended label, provider details, the support workflow, and policy text using [app-privacy.md](app-privacy.md).
