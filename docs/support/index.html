PicLayout support

Try an example project

Choose Open an example on Home and choose a design. PicLayout creates an editable copy with bundled artwork. You can try the editor, change the layout, and export a collage without using your own photos.

Save or share a collage

Open the editor’s export button, choose PNG or JPEG and an image size, then save to Photos or open the share sheet. If saving to Photos is denied, allow PicLayout to add photos in iOS Settings or choose a destination from the share sheet.

Move an editable project

Choose Export project from the project menu and save the .piclayout file to Files. Use Import project on Home to reopen a copy. The archive includes the project’s images.

Contact support

In PicLayout, open Settings and choose Report a problem. Include your app version, device and iOS version, and the steps that led to the problem. Share personal photos or project files only if you choose to.
