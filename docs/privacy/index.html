Privacy policy

This policy applies to the PicLayout app and its support and privacy pages.

Photos and projects

PicLayout edits photos and renders collages on your device. When you choose images in the system photo picker, PicLayout copies the selected images into its local project storage. Your original photos remain unchanged. Projects, edits, templates, and preferences are stored locally. Your operating system may include eligible app files in device backups according to your settings.

Photo access and saving

On iOS, the system picker provides the images you select. PicLayout requests permission to add an image to Photos when you choose to save an export there. Change that permission in iOS Settings. You can also share or save an export through the system share sheet.

Project archives and sharing

An editable .piclayout archive includes project details and copies of the project images. When you import an archive using the system file picker, PicLayout creates a local project copy. When you share an image or archive, the operating system passes that file to the destination you select. The destination app or provider handles any subsequent storage, upload, or publication under its own privacy policy.

Data collection and tracking

The PicLayout app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking. Its editor and project storage do not send photos, projects, or usage information to the PicLayout publisher. If you contact support, your email address and the information you choose to send are used to respond to your request. The in-app problem report opens a draft containing the app version and generic platform information. You can review it before sending.

These pages use no analytics scripts, advertising, or site cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the website and processes technical data such as visitor IP addresses under its own privacy policy. GitHub privacy statement

Retention and deletion

Delete a project from its menu to remove its local project files and copies. Clear temporary exports from Settings. Deleting the app removes its local app container. Photos saved to Photos, exported files, copies held by other apps, and operating system backups are managed separately. You can ask the publisher about retention or request deletion of support correspondence at the contact below.
